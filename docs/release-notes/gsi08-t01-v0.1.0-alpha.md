**Research and experimental alpha**

These notes prepare the first research alpha release of GSI-08 T01.

This release is a standalone Python simulator for studying how operations by multiple agents affect shared resources and how safe admission control can be designed.

It is provided as a research implementation that does not assume dependence on a particular AI model, developer, or cloud service.

## Main implementation features

The release maintainer describes the candidate as implementing:

- Compositional safety assessment for shared resources
- Consistency checks for plans and evidence
- STOP, DENY, HOLD, and ALLOW decisions
- Atomic admission and resource reservation within a single process
- Simulated approvals and authority-epoch checks
- Duplicate-operation prevention
- Consistency checks between audit history and admission receipts
- FH01 correction to the precedence of definite FAIL results
- Verification tests covering 300 concurrent-admission trials

## Known issues and limitations

This alpha has the following unresolved or unverified items.

- **Audit history (AUDIT02):** Detecting audit corruption does not automatically stop subsequent admission of otherwise valid, approved operations.
- **Evidence:** Semantic consistency checks for unknown evidence are limited.
- **STOP release and recovery:** Authority management with independent authentication and approval is incomplete.
- **Simulated approvals:** Guarantees concerning ownership of approval information and concurrent processing are limited.
- **Verification coverage:** Some internal tests do not check the actual admission decision.
- **Performance:** Performance and processing times for large inputs or long-running execution are not guaranteed.

These items remain tracked as known limitations. Unresolved issues are not treated as fixed.

## Future development

Further work will consider improving the precision of safety verification, investigating known issues, and extending the scope of what can be verified.

Current priorities are:

- Appropriate application of STOP conditions and management of recovery authority
- Prevention of admission of unapproved operations
- Prevention of invalid resource reservations and duplicate admissions
- Better consistency between audit information and admission control
- Improved reproducibility and reliability of verification results

Display, diagnostics, performance, and development support may also be improved as needed.

**All future development scope, implementation approaches, priorities, and release timing are current plans only.**

They may change in response to new verification results, technical challenges, research progress, or development circumstances. No commitment is made to implementing or delivering any listed feature or improvement, or to a particular release date.
